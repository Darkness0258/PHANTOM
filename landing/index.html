Capabilities

Network Map

Visual real-time map of all devices on your network. See who's connected, what they're doing, and flag unknown intruders instantly.

Threat Detection

Automatic detection of port scans, ARP spoofing, and unknown device connections with instant push alerts.

Desktop Agent

Native Electron app for Windows. Runs silently in the system tray, monitoring your network 24/7 without interrupting your workflow.

Mobile App

Android companion app lets you monitor your network from anywhere. Get alerts, manage devices, and control settings on the go.

Parental Controls

Block websites, set screen time limits, and monitor your children's browsing activity with easy per-device management.

Phantom AI

Built-in AI assistant powered by Groq. Ask questions about your network, get threat analysis, and receive security recommendations.
